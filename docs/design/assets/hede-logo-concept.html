The idea

You take the helm.

HeDE is the desktop you steer your Gentoo system from — so the mark is the ship's wheel. It carries the H of HeDE cut clean through the hub, and it's staged the way early-90s software met the sea: a brass helm adrift in a starfield, above a glowing curved horizon. Warm hand-turned metal against a cold CRT sky — that contrast is the identity.

The system

One mark, every surface.

The helm holds up from a login splash down to a 24-pixel favicon, because the circular silhouette and the hub-H read at any size.

Construction

Built on a compass rose.

  • 8×Eight spokes & handles on 45° bearings — the traditional deck helm, and a nod to a compass rose.
  • HHub cutout is the letter H, knocked straight through the metal so the wordmark lives inside the mark.
  • 3:1Handles clear the rim — the protruding grips are what make a wheel read as a ship's wheel, not a gear.
  • ◐Bevel, not gloss — a single top-left highlight and a shadow edge. Warm brass, never chrome.
Palette

Brass on the abyss.

One warm family for the metal, one cold ground, and a single teal accent — the horizon glow — used sparingly so it always means "the sea meets the sky."

Usage

Keep it seaworthy.

↔Clearspace

Keep clear space of one hub-diameter on every side. The handles are part of the mark — never crop them.

▭Minimum size

24px for the favicon, 32px in a panel. Below that, drop to the flat silhouette — the brass bevel muddies.

⊘Don't re-hue the brass

The metal stays in its warm family. Recolour only the flat mark, and only to one solid ink.

◱Grounds

Prefer the abyss. On light grounds use the knockout mark; never set full-colour brass on white.
