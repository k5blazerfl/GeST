Helm · Desktop · Environment
The idea

A helm, cut like a gear.

HeDE is the desktop you steer your Gentoo system from — so the mark is the ship's wheel. But this is a desktop environment, kin to KDE, so the wheel is machined into a cog: gear teeth on the rim, spokes to the hub, and the H of HeDE knocked clean through the centre. Cool pewter, not warm brass — a mechanism, precise and engineered, still adrift in the old starfield.

The system

One mark, every surface.

The cog-helm holds up from a login splash down to a 24-pixel favicon — the toothed silhouette and the hub-H stay legible at any size.

Construction

Eight teeth on the rose.

  • 8×Eight gear teeth on 45° bearings — a cog for a desktop environment, and the eight bearings of a compass rose.
  • HHub cutout is the letter H, at the same weight as the teeth, so the wordmark lives inside the mechanism — KDE's trick with the K.
  • ↺Spokes remain — teeth alone make a gear; the spokes to the hub are what keep it a ship's wheel.
  • ◐Machined, not glossy — a single cool highlight and a dark edge. Pewter reads as tooling, not treasure.
Palette

Pewter on the abyss.

One cool metal family for the mechanism, one deep ground, and a single teal accent — the horizon glow — used sparingly so it always means "the sea meets the sky."

Usage

Keep it seaworthy.

↔Clearspace

Keep clear space of one hub-diameter on every side. The teeth are part of the mark — never crop them.

▭Minimum size

24px for the favicon, 32px in a panel. Below that, drop to the flat silhouette — the pewter bevel muddies.

⊘Keep the pewter neutral

The cog stays a cool grey. Recolour only the flat mark, and only to one solid ink.

◱Grounds

Prefer the abyss. On light grounds use the flat mark in ink; reserve the full pewter bevel for dark or mid grounds.
