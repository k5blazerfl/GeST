The idea

You take the helm.

HeDE is the desktop you steer your Gentoo system from — so the mark is the ship's wheel: warm bronze, hand-turned, adrift in an early-90s starfield above a glowing horizon. The wheel stays clean and uncluttered; the H of HeDE rides at the lower right as a captain's seal, notched into the rim so it reads like a stamp on the wheel — a monogram, not a hubcap.

The system

One mark, every surface.

The wheel-and-seal holds up from a login splash down to a 48-pixel icon. At the very smallest sizes, drop to the flat mark so the seal stays crisp.

Construction

Wheel centred, seal at 4 o'clock.

  • 8×Eight spokes & handles on 45° bearings — the traditional deck helm, and a nod to a compass rose.
  • HThe seal sits lower-right (on the 4-o'clock diagonal), so the monogram is off the wheel's face and the spokes stay legible.
  • ◌A notched moat knocks the wheel out around the seal, so the badge separates cleanly on any ground — starfield or solid.
  • ◐Bevel, not gloss — one warm top-left highlight and a shadow edge. Bronze, never chrome.

Usage

Keep it seaworthy.

↔Clearspace

Keep clear space of one hub-diameter on every side. The handles and the seal are part of the mark — never crop them.

▭Minimum size

32px in a panel, 48px for the full bronze. At favicon sizes, use the flat mark — the bevel muddies below that.

⊘Don't re-hue the bronze

The metal stays in its warm family. Recolour only the flat mark, and only to one solid ink.

◱Grounds

Prefer the abyss. On light grounds use the knockout mark; never set full-colour bronze on white.
